# Refund & Cancellation Policy

If you cancel a booking at least 24 hours before the shift starts, the amount you paid will be returned within 7 business days.

This policy forms part of our [Terms & Conditions](/terms-and-conditions). RestoCare is operated by Restroedge Private Limited.

## 1. How to cancel

Contact us on WhatsApp, phone or email (details at the end of this policy) and tell us which booking you want to cancel. We use the time we receive your request to work out whether you cancelled at least 24 hours before the shift starts.

## 2. Refunds for cancellations

- **Cancelled at least 24 hours before the shift starts:** the amount you paid is returned within 7 business days.
- **Cancelled closer to the start time:** please contact us as soon as you can. We'll look at your booking and tell you what we can do.

## 3. When we or the professional can't deliver

If we cancel your booking, or we can't provide a professional for it, we'll tell you as soon as we know. You'll get a full refund, or a replacement professional if you prefer.

## 4. How refunds are paid

- Refunds go back to the payment method you used.
- Refunds are returned within 7 business days of a valid cancellation or of us approving your refund.
- Refunds are based on what you actually paid, after any discount. Coupons have no cash value.

## 5. Failed or double payments

Sometimes money leaves your account but no booking is confirmed, or you're charged twice. If this happens, contact us with the transaction reference and we'll look into it.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version on this page. Changes don't affect bookings made before the update.
