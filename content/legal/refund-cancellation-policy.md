# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last updated:** [[CONFIRM: date of publishing]]

This policy explains when you can cancel a booking or order on RestoCare, what you get back, and how long refunds take. It forms part of our [Terms & Conditions](/terms-and-conditions). RestoCare is operated by Restroedge Private Limited.

The version of this policy in force when you made a booking is the one that applies to that booking.

## At a glance: staffing and deep cleaning bookings

| When you cancel | What you get back |
|---|---|
| Within 15 minutes of paying, if the booking starts more than 4 hours later | Full refund |
| More than 24 hours before the start time | Full refund |
| Between 4 and 24 hours before the start time | 75% of the amount paid |
| Less than 4 hours before the start time | 50% of the amount paid |
| After the start time, or if the professional arrives but can't start work for reasons on your side | No refund |
| We cancel, the professional cancels, or no one turns up | Full refund, or a replacement professional if you prefer |

Percentages apply to the total you paid for the booking, including GST.

## 1. Cancelling a staffing booking

This covers chefs, kitchen helpers, waiters, and utility and housekeeping staff booked by the shift or by the week.

**How to cancel.** [[CONFIRM: describe the in-app/website cancel path, e.g. "Open the booking under My Bookings and tap Cancel". If there is no self-serve cancel button yet, say so and keep only the contact route.]] You can also cancel by contacting us on WhatsApp, phone or email (details at the end of this policy).

**When a cancellation counts.** We use the time we receive your request: the time you cancel in the app, or the time your message or email reaches us. This applies outside our support hours and on Sundays too.

**Why there's a fee closer to the shift.** Once a professional is assigned, they keep that time free for you and usually turn down other work. The closer to the start time you cancel, the harder it is for them to fill it.

**Rescheduling.** Moving a booking to a new date or time is free if you ask more than 24 hours before the original start time and we can staff the new slot. Inside 24 hours, a change is treated as a cancellation under the table above plus a new booking.

**Weekly bookings.** If you cancel the rest of a weekly booking:

- Days that start more than 24 hours after your request are refunded in full.
- A day that starts within 24 hours of your request follows the table above.
- Days already worked are not refunded.

## 2. Cancelling a deep cleaning booking

Deep cleaning bookings follow the same cancellation table as staffing bookings.

If that happens, they'll tell you before starting and explain the difference in price. You can pay the difference through RestoCare and they'll carry on. ## 3. When we or the professional can't deliver

**If we can't staff your booking, or the professional cancels.** We'll tell you as soon as we know and offer a replacement professional with suitable skills. If you don't want a replacement, or we can't find one in time, you get a full refund.

**If the professional is late.** If they arrive more than 60 minutes after the start time without having agreed a delay with you, you can:

- cancel for a full refund, or
- keep the booking. If the shift can't run for the full booked duration, we'll refund the hours missed.

**If the professional leaves early or works fewer hours than booked** and it wasn't your request, we'll refund the hours not worked. We work out the hourly amount by dividing the amount you paid by the number of hours booked.

**If the professional can't do the role you booked.** Tell us within the first hour of the shift. We'll try to arrange a replacement. If we can't, we'll refund the hours not worked. If the problem made the hours that were worked unusable to you, tell us and we'll review a full refund.

## 4. Complaints about the quality of a service

- **Staffing:** tell us within 24 hours of the shift ending.
- **Deep cleaning:** tell us within 48 hours of the job being completed, with photos if you can.

We'll review what happened with you and the professional. Depending on what we find, we may offer a repeat service (for deep cleaning), a replacement professional on a future booking, a partial or full refund, or a credit on your account.

Damage to your property or belongings is covered in section 11 of our [Terms & Conditions](/terms-and-conditions). Please report it within 24 hours.

## 5. Product orders (cleaning chemicals and kitchen supplies)

**Cancelling.** You can cancel an order for a full refund any time before it's dispatched. Once it's dispatched, it can't be cancelled, but the return rules below still apply.

**Returns.** We'll replace an item or refund it if it:

- arrives damaged or leaking,
- is the wrong product or the wrong quantity, or
- is past its use-by or expiry date when delivered.

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the product, its label and the packaging.

**What we can't take back.** For safety reasons, we can't accept returns of chemical products that have been opened or used, or that weren't stored as the label directs after delivery. We also don't accept returns when you've changed your mind about a product that arrived as ordered.

**Delivery charges.** These are refunded only when the return is because of our mistake: damaged, wrong or expired goods.

## 6. How refunds are paid

**Where the money goes.** Refunds go back to the payment method you used: the same UPI account, card or bank account.

**How long it takes.** We start the refund within 2 business days of the cancellation or of approving your request. After that, it usually reaches you within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank. UPI refunds are often faster.

**GST and invoices.** If we issued you a GST tax invoice, we'll also issue a credit note for the refunded amount, so your input tax credit stays correct. Cancellation fees include GST at the applicable rate.

**Coupons and offers.** Refunds are based on what you actually paid after any discount. A discount has no cash value and isn't refunded as money. If *we* cancel a booking made with a first-booking offer, we'll restore the offer so you can use it again. If *you* cancel, a one-time offer may not be restored.

**Failed or double payments.** Sometimes money leaves your account but no booking is confirmed, or you're charged twice. Your bank or our payment partner reverses these automatically, usually within 5–7 business days. If you haven't received it after 7 business days, contact us with the transaction reference and we'll chase it.

**Chargebacks.** Please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank. We can usually sort things out much faster than the chargeback process.
